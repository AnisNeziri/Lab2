## DC39.1 technical evidence

- Supplier matching now checks accepted quantity on each receipt line, combines repeated claims within a bill, and rechecks previously posted claims at approval. A draft or repeated approval does not create a second payable. Other legacy bills without a definite receipt link still require investigation. Confirm the source and tax treatment against the original invoice and acceptance documents.
- The synthetic import crossed two receipts, two foreign-currency supplier invoices, an advance and settlements, customs VAT, late freight after a partial sale, and a partial customer payment. The ledger reported €138 inventory, €92 cost of goods sold, €50.80 receivables, €36 payables, €1,862.50 cash and bank, and €40.50 net result. AIMS used separately recorded historical invoice and settlement exchange rates and recognized a €7.50 realized loss. The freight allocation uses the more precise receipt share to split €37.50 to inventory and €12.50 to cost of goods sold. The synthetic bank statement independently tied to €1,762.50 across six matched lines with zero difference and no new journals. The €100 till balance completes the combined cash and bank amount. The test set up the opening bank balance through the existing accounting service.
- The exercise covered internal statement and VAT control totals. Approved opening evidence, customs duty and insurance evidence, foreign-currency customer billing and period-end revaluation, a fixed-asset register and depreciation, inventory impairment, and an approved year-end close remain open. Contract-specific sales recognition requires approved delivery terms and evidence of when control transfers.
