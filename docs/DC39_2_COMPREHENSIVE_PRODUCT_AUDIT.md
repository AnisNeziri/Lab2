## Accounting control findings

1. **P0 annual close without a tax decision.** `backend/app/Services/FinancialControlService.php:246–249` only adds the current-tax check if a worksheet exists. `backend/app/Services/Kosovo/CorporateTaxReviewService.php:110–119` returns true when no worksheet exists. An isolated Kosovo synthetic company with initialized books and zero reconciliation discrepancies received HTTP 200 and period state `closed` with no worksheet or approved applicability decision. The toolchain cannot distinguish a legitimate exemption from omission. Require recorded jurisdiction/regime/applicability with an auditable approved exemption path and verify every close entry point.
2. **P0 stale source approval with unchanged profit.** `CorporateTaxReviewService.php:58–64` fingerprints summary inputs. A synthetic approved worksheet retained its fingerprint and passed `tax_review_current` after adding equal €100 income and potentially nondeductible €100 expense. A stable source-evidence digest must detect composition changes, including approved policy and adjustments. Target both routes and reopened periods. Both probes passed their reproductions, 2 tests and 12 assertions.
3. **P0 chart-of-accounts collision.** `AccountingService.php:60–68,79–86` defines 6500 and 6510 for asset depreciation and impairment. `Workforce/PayrollService.php:34,58` reserves those codes for salary and employer pension expense and correctly rejects repurposing. All six failing payroll tests reproduced on SQLite and MariaDB, with HTTP 422. An additional isolated payroll-first chart probe (1 test, 3 assertions) established that accounting initialization silently mapped asset depreciation and impairment to Salary Expense and Employer Pension Expense. Separate the two sets of default accounts and audit previously affected mappings without silently rewriting recorded journals.
4. **Assets.** `FixedAssetService.php:109–137` ties bill capitalization and reversal to approved asset treatment. The existing tests cover depreciation, impairment, disposal, invoice sale and credit reversal, tenant and period permissions. `FixedAssetService.php:246–359` covers schedule and register evidence. Still qualify a dated concurrent batch, subsequent-cost VAT/disposal corrections, original data mapping and separate tax depreciation.
