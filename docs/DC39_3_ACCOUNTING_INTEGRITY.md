## Annual evidence and restoration

- The [additive migration](../backend/database/migrations/2026_11_25_000001_add_corporate_tax_dispositions.php) creates company-scoped disposition history and initializes a reopen revision to zero. It does not populate policies, approve exemptions, change account codes or update journals. Reopening increments the revision, even when requests share a timestamp. Final close and worksheet approval use the existing company lock. Ordinary monthly periods do not require an annual tax disposition.
- The built-in calculation covers full January–December years for a recorded Kosovo entity with EUR books. A full annual range in an unsupported jurisdiction, currency or calendar receives no automatic exemption. The alternative review requires an accountant-entered accounting basis, policy and posted amount. The service does not calculate statutory turnover or foreign regimes.
- The dated evidence stream is canonical by journal-line ID and document-link ID, hashed in chunks. It records ledger classification and amount, source identifiers, journal revisions, issued-invoice checksums, and currently linked invoice, expense and journal document versions/status. Reversal entries enter the digest in their accounting period. A subsequent-period reversal does not rewrite the earlier period's basis. Physical document bytes and salaries are not exposed in the readiness response. Outside-company and outside-period posting controls are covered.
- Only journals explicitly linked to reviewed tax deltas are excluded from calculation input. Their source keys, posting date, accounts, state and balances are checked independently. The service verifies the total current tax in the P&L before reapproval. It rejects mismatched journal evidence. An identical approval returns the same record. A revised approval posts only the difference between the previously recorded and newly approved tax. The tests independently cover €10 initial tax, €8 added tax to reach €18, and an €8 reduction back to €10. An approved €0 result posts no zero-value journal.
- [Portable recovery](../backend/app/Services/PortableBackupService.php) preserves archived disposition and worksheet evidence, maps approver, period, journal, source and posting account IDs, and marks both reviews historical without replacing the original hash. The encrypted synthetic restore covers wrong-passphrase rejection, unchanged source, remapped references and reapproval on the destination without duplicate tax. Installed backup and rollback remain DC41 gates.

## Final pre-commit review

The 30 application and test files initially matched the successful full-regression manifest byte for byte. A fresh isolated probe reproduced a separate close-classification defect. A December-only range reached the configured year-end, received the interim status and finalized with HTTP 200 and no tax disposition.

The sole final period-close route continues to require readiness under the existing company lock. The pre-commit correction derives the scope from the configured fiscal calendar. Final closure at a fiscal-year boundary requires an exact full fiscal year and then its applicable tax evidence. A partial or extended range that reaches or crosses that boundary cannot receive final close or approve a disposition. Soft close remains available for adjustments. A range wholly before the boundary can close as interim only, with that scope in its proof regardless of the request name or payload. Reports can still be produced by date. They do not create close evidence. Accounting settings prevent changing the fiscal start once periods have been created.

For a fiscal year already partitioned into monthly periods, the existing non-overlap requirement still applies. The final month can be soft-closed, and a qualified annual close requires an accountant-reviewed workflow that represents the whole fiscal year without rewriting financial history. Short and irregular fiscal years have no qualified annual-close path. No further migration or historical data update is required by this review correction.

Eight application and test files differ from the original full-regression manifest. On the final source, 128 affected backend tests passed on SQLite with 1,533 assertions and 128 passed on MariaDB with 1,532 assertions. Neither affected run had failures or skips. All 113 frontend tests and the production build passed. An Albanian mobile accountant session showed the final-close block and the API returned HTTP 422. Soft close succeeded without a final-close snapshot. The historical full-suite runs were retained separately. Final-source logs and the exact proposed file list are in the local evidence root under `precommit/`.

The proposed file set includes the unchanged DC39.2 historical audit so its links resolve. The file review excluded private data, environment files, credentials, database fixtures and generated artifacts. Local commit review is complete. Required hosted jobs remain pending on the exact owner-reviewed commit.
