## Completion report

1. **Employee/employment:** Employee 360, optional existing-user link, department/position/manager, lifecycle, termination with reason and optional permission-checked access revocation. Employees and users remain separate. Historical employees cannot be deleted. Employment terms include contract dates/type, position, department, probation, schedule, notes and documents.
2. **Compensation history:** Approved immutable versions explicitly supersede previous terms from a date. Monthly/hourly/daily bases, primary/secondary employer and pension treatment are recorded. Each payroll freezes its applicable terms. Partial monthly compensation uses an explicitly approved **calendar-day company policy**, not an invented legal entitlement.
3. **Schedule/attendance/leave:** Immutable reusable weekly schedules, generic shift assignments, authorized attendance, leave request/decision/taken history, and configured entitlement/used/planned/remaining. Annual leave crossing entitlement years must be split. Salaried paid leave preserves monthly pay; unpaid leave needs an explicitly reviewed gross reduction. No biometric or hardware integration.
4. **Additions/deductions/advances:** Approved bonuses, overtime with hours and explicit pay-policy basis, authorized deductions, paid advances and approved payroll repayment instructions. Outstanding advances are derived from actual disbursements less repayments in posted payroll. No independent cash or accounting engine.
5. **Calculation:** Decimal gross → employee pension → taxable pay → withholding → other deductions/advance repayment → net. Employer pension remains a separate employer cost. Net → gross is a planning tool. Changed salaries, pending inputs and unusual values produce explicit review notices; missing terms/rules/mappings or unsupported treatments block calculation/posting.
6. **Kosovo statutory rules:** One versioned configuration, frozen on payroll lines. The reviewed ordinary monthly rule uses 0% through €250 taxable pay, 8% from €250 to €450 and 10% above €450; secondary employer withholding is 10%. Employee and employer pension rates are separately validated at 5–15%. Temporary foreign **non-resident** exclusion requires an explicit documented basis and zero contributions; citizenship alone never causes automatic exemption. Other residency/retirement/exemption cases require review. See [official sources](OFFICIAL_SOURCES.json).
7. **Review/approval/posting:** Calculate → review → approve the fingerprint → post. Relevant source changes invalidate unposted approval; explicit recalculation also revokes approval. Posting rechecks current inputs under the existing company lock. Retries return the original posting. Paid/posted snapshots cannot be edited. Unpaid payroll can be reversed with a reason/date and linked journal, then corrected in a new run revision.
8. **Accounting integration:** Existing `AccountingService`, Chart of Accounts/posting mappings and period controls remain authoritative. Dedicated configurable salary, pension expense/payable, withholding, deduction and employee advance accounts. Payroll controls reconcile to GL centrally and per run. Month close blocks missing/unposted payroll and control differences. Generic journals/bank reversal cannot bypass payroll ownership. Installation backup reconciliation includes workforce counts and controls when workforce data exists.
9. **Salary payment:** Explicit per-employee partial/full salary settlement and run-level tax/pension/deduction settlement use `FinancialAccountService`. No repeated salary expense. Idempotency keys reject conflicting retries. Payment/advance history links to journals. CSV assists payment preparation; it is **not a bank integration**.
10. **Payslip:** Confidential EN/SQ posted payslips with earnings, employee pension, withholding, deductions, advance repayment, net, employer contribution/cost and payment balance. Print/save-PDF uses the browser. Linked employees can read only their own posted payroll, profile, schedule, leave and attendance. Bank/identity snapshots are encrypted and excluded from ordinary API output.
11. **Obligations/declarations:** Accruals, settlements, outstanding salary/tax/employee pension/employer pension/deduction liabilities and GL differences. WM/CM preparation CSV uses frozen employee identity and rule evidence; missing reviewed identifiers block export. The nominal 1–15 following-month deadline is qualified for official non-working-day extensions and actual payment-period review. **No automatic EDI filing or verified official upload adapter.** Accrual reconciliation is not evidence that a statutory return was filed or accepted.
12. **Permissions/privacy:** Separate employee, compensation, HR-document, workforce approval, payroll view/manage/approve/post/pay/reverse/configure and own-portal permissions. New HR role; ordinary manager/staff roles receive no salary access. API/tenant/own-payslip checks, private no-store responses, guarded journal/account activity/GL export/bank details, persistent document privacy after unlink, restricted notifications and redacted generic payroll audit values. `workforce:setup` preserves existing grants and revocations.
13. **Ask AIMS:** Authorized read-only EN/SQ workforce/payroll summaries, active headcount, named pending leave/overtime requests and bounded unpaid salary lists. No posting, salary change, payment, leave approval, firing or employee-ranking action. Action Center receives bounded meaningful requests, contract review and payroll review items. Employee leave updates and payroll review notices use existing notifications.

15. **Remaining gaps:** Live payroll pilot is **NO** pending accountant/company-policy validation. Review statutory rounding at boundaries, residency/pension age/special exemptions, benefits in kind, minimum-wage/employment policy, deductions, final pay and legal entitlements. Hourly/daily paid-leave earnings, supplemental/combined arrears and settled-payroll refunds/corrections are explicitly unsupported. Multiple earnings periods for one employee in one payment month are blocked rather than applying tax bands twice. No official EDI format certification or bank connection. Selective portable company exports containing workforce/finance/documents are blocked for workforce companies: frozen payroll identity/reference remapping is unsupported; use the complete encrypted installation backup and preserve its application key.
